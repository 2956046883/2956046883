## 🔬 Research Interests
My research focuses on the intersection of **cryptography, information theory, and multi-agent systems**, with a particular emphasis on designing efficient and secure protocols for large-scale distributed networks.

- **Cryptography & Information Theory**  
  Graph secret sharing, random geometric graphs, information rate optimization, distributed cryptographic systems, asymptotic analysis of network protocols

- **Multi-Agent Cooperative Control**  
  Model predictive control (MPC), swarm intelligence, asymmetric defense strategies, real-time decision-making systems

- **Network Security**  
  Topology analysis of complex networks, distributed system security, AI-enabled cybersecurity
